# SEO work and next steps

Implemented locally: descriptive recruitment title and description, canonical URL, robots directives, Open Graph/Twitter previews, Organization/WebSite/Service JSON-LD, sitemap and robots.txt. Existing static service text is readable without executing JavaScript. The company serves India; “Growing beyond borders” expresses expansion intent, not a claim of current overseas operations.

Primary focus: specialist recruitment for marketing, creative, design and technology; brands and agencies; Mumbai and India. Avoid claiming active AI matching while the implementation remains a capability preview. No invented reviews, performance statistics, locations or global coverage are included in structured data.

After an authorised deployment:
1. Verify qleaps.in in Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml. Check homepage indexing and query/impression trends.
2. Confirm an accurate Google Business Profile if eligible, and consistent business details across legitimate directories and company profiles.
3. Add permissioned hiring case studies, actual consultant expertise and useful role-specific employer guidance to the page. These provide substance beyond slogans.
4. Earn relevant links through real partnerships, published expertise and industry participation. Avoid purchased link schemes.
5. Monitor mobile Core Web Vitals and organic employer enquiries. Reduce animation/image costs if real-user performance suffers.

A single page can support a focused set of searches; ranking broadly across every role, location and global market is a larger content task. Meta tags alone cannot guarantee outranking competitors. The current JS-rendered job list has no durable individual job URLs, so JobPosting markup is deliberately omitted until individual, current, indexable job pages exist.

References: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization
