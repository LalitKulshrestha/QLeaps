Tips for your next move.

Before you say yes

Four questions to ask before accepting an offer.

The right offer is about more than the number.
Who will I report to, and how do they like to work?
What does success look like in my first 90 days?
Why is this role open right now?
Where can this role lead in two years?
Designers and creatives

Your portfolio should show how you think.

Hiring managers spend minutes, not hours. Make them count.
Lead with your three strongest projects
Show the problem, your process and the result
Keep it current, and trim the rest
Serving notice

Make the most of a long notice period.

Sixty to ninety days is a long time. Use it well.
Stay in touch with your new manager
Ask about the first projects you’ll work on
Hand over cleanly, so you leave on good terms
