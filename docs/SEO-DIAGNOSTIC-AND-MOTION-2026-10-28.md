# SEO, diagnostic restoration and ambient motion

Local review and implementation — 28 October 2026. No deployment, Google submission or payment action.

## What changed

- Services, Knowledge and For Candidates have different decorative CSS/SVG background paths: connected tracks, reading lines and stepped paths. No WebGL, remote animation asset, extra framework or server was introduced. Backgrounds are non-interactive and hidden from assistive technology; motion respects reduced motion, tab visibility and offscreen state.
- The HR tool is now explicitly named **HR Maturity & Compliance Diagnostic**. It is available on Home, Services, People Setup and Hiring Support. The hero, Services opening and both relevant service pages link directly to it.
- Four native sliders with equivalent select controls cover team size, current HR owner, reported POSH status and offer-to-joining dropouts. No default answers are silently counted. Keyboard slider operation and reset were tested.
- A complete answer set gives an **indicative stage**, independent findings and an exact workstream roadmap. Team size changes context only. It never raises the stage by itself. A reported POSH gap is never cancelled out by good hiring answers.
- Stage 1 means foundations to build, Stage 2 means setup to strengthen, and Stage 3 means maintenance/review. These are rule-based product labels, not validated maturity benchmarks or legal conclusions. No percentage of compliance, legal liability conclusion or guarantee is generated.
- The CTA passes only whitelisted roadmap identifiers and a service category to a reviewable enquiry. The draft contains workstream titles, not team-size bands, HR owner status, reported committee status or dropout estimates. Nothing is automatically submitted. Existing Google Apps Script needs no update.

## SEO verdict

The local static build meets the checked technical SEO foundations. “SEO compliant” is not a ranking certification: public accessibility, server responses, indexing and real performance still need live verification.

Checked across 29 HTML pages:

- Unique titles and unique descriptions; no missing description.
- One H1 per page, canonical URLs, valid JSON-LD and sitemap entries.
- Crawlable internal links and locally published resources; no missing anchors in the contract tests.
- Only the 404 page is marked noindex. Public robots file allows crawling; internal folders are excluded from publication.
- All image elements have alt attributes. This check does not establish the quality of every description.
- No lifetime offer copy; current offer is ₹3,999 one-time package, reference price ₹5,999.

Google states that meeting technical requirements makes a page eligible, not guaranteed to be indexed: [Google Search technical requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/technical).

## Is the content/data good?

The scope is coherent with the five service lines, samples are explicitly illustrative, and the diagnostic is explicitly a planning tool. There are no invented client outcome statistics or AI matching claims on the public landing/service copy. Keep the reference price and limited-period offer grounded in a real commercial decision; the actual offer deadline, revision limits, delivery arrangements and refund/tax terms remain owner decisions before taking payment.

The diagnostic asks about POSH status without pretending it can establish compliance. It recommends a qualified applicability/composition review rather than prescribing that every external member must be an advocate. The official Act describes the external-member category more broadly: [India Code — official Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15239/1/sexual_harassment_act_2013.pdf). The tool does not replace engagement-specific advice.

## Remaining improvements, in priority order

1. Verify indexing and selected canonicals in Search Console after an authorised deployment. This local audit cannot establish what Google currently indexes.
2. Add approved delivery-team facts and real evidence as engagements occur. Working samples are useful proof of approach but not client references.
3. Develop practitioner-reviewed People Setup/Hiring Support guides; current articles remain weighted toward recruitment/careers. Avoid generic location pages or legal claims created just for keywords.
4. Approved jobs currently rely on the browser-loaded feed. For job-search visibility, separately plan crawlable job-detail URLs with accurate expiry and application behaviour before adding JobPosting schema. Account for Netlify build-credit implications.
5. Measure production mobile LCP, INP and CLS and slow-network poster/video behaviour. No Lighthouse score or ranking gain is claimed here.
6. A discreet global motion setting remains recommended for accessibility. Existing requests to remove the visible pause button have been respected; reduced motion is present but not a full substitute for user control.
