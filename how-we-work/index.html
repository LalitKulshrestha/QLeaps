The Quantum Leaps Difference

Three checks that make hiring decisions clearer.

Standard 01

Strict Pre-Screening

We discuss relevant work, responsibilities and motivation with candidates before making an introduction. We highlight what is understood and what still needs checking.

Standard 02

Compensation & expectations

We break down current CTC into fixed base, performance bonus, and unvested equity before scheduling first client interviews. Candidates are pre-aligned on the role's budget band.

Standard 03

Notice Period Governance

Long notice periods can introduce uncertainty. We discuss availability, maintain agreed touchpoints and flag changes in expectations before joining.
