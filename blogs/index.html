Executive Summary & Recommended Actions

Four Questions to Ask Before Accepting an Offer: Due Diligence for High-Growth Leaders

The right executive offer is about more than the headline number. Clarifying reporting lines, 90-day success metrics, and mandate reasons prevents career misalignment.

Offer Due Diligence Role Alignment Leadership Cadence

Four Critical Due Diligence Areas

  • Who will I report to, and how do they like to work? Confirm reporting structure, autonomy level, and executive sponsor expectations.
  • What does success look like in my first 90 days? Ensure the mandate has clear deliverables rather than vague exploratory charters.
  • Why is this role open right now? Learn if this is new growth capacity, backfill, or strategic restructuring.
  • Where can this role lead in two years? Understand company roadmap, funding runway, and organizational growth trajectory.
