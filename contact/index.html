Before we talk

A few useful answers.

Can you help with specialist roles as well as leadership?

Yes. We support marketing, creative, design and technology hiring, from specialist contributors to team and functional leaders. Tell us the responsibilities, location and budget so we can discuss the scope.

Can I send a job description?

Use the detailed brief above to attach a PDF or Word file up to 5 MB. Include a short role summary so we understand what matters before opening the attachment.

Will my contact details or attached JD be public?

Employer contact details and JD files stay private to our recruitment team. Only approved job summaries appear in the public roles list. We review briefs before publication.

I’m looking for a job. Where should I start?
